Identity information deserves special care

Reduce what you collect.
Control what you share.

Identity documents, dates of birth, health records and payment details deserve more than another inbox or upload folder.

LXDiN is designed to reduce unnecessary copies and manual handling, with purpose-specific authorization and an auditable handoff.

  • Verification results instead of raw identity documents where permitted.
  • Separate custody and access workflows for health documents.
  • Payment references instead of storing card details.
  • Defined access, expiration and exception handling.

Risk is reduced by validated controls, not eliminated. Account compromise, provider custody and integration errors remain risks. Revocation cannot recall information already lawfully received. No compliance certification is claimed.
